The Vanik Matrimonial Register is a not-for-profit, volunteer-run matrimonial register for adults from Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, costing £10 a year. Below are answers to the questions singles and parents ask most often. If yours is not here, email matrimonial@vanikcouncil.uk.

## Joining

### Who can join the Vanik Matrimonial Register?

Membership is open to adults aged 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families. The register is run by Vanik Council volunteers in the UK and has helped families find respectful introductions for over 40 years.

### Do I have to be from a Vanik family?

No. The register is open to all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, whether or not your family is Vanik.

### Is it only for people in London?

No. The register is for Gujarati Hindu and Jain families wherever they live in the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too. Every profile shows where someone lives and any plans they have about where to settle. See our guide to [matches beyond London](/guides/matches-beyond-london).

### I am in my 20s. Is the register for me?

Yes. Anyone aged 18 or over from a Gujarati Hindu or Jain family can join. Many people begin thinking about marriage in their 20s, and the register is a calm, low-pressure way to start. Read our guide on [finding a partner in your 20s](/guides/finding-a-partner-in-your-20s).

### How do I register?

Go to [the registration page](/register), create an account with your email address and confirm it. You then complete a short form in three parts: identity and address, personal details, and your profile and photos. It only takes a few minutes. The full process is on [how it works](/how-it-works).

### What information will I be asked for?

You will be asked for your gender, date of birth, mobile number, home address, name, nationality, where you live, your family's town and country of origin, religion, education, job title, height, diet, and hobbies and interests. You also upload up to three photos and proof of identity. Future settlement plans and a LinkedIn profile are optional.

### What proof of identity do I need?

The photo page of your passport, or your driving licence, uploaded as a JPG or PNG image. It is used only to verify your identity and is deleted after approval.

### What photos should I upload?

You can upload up to three photos, and we recommend all three. Your main photo should be clear and recent, with your face easy to see. Head and shoulders works best. Full-length shots are fine as extra photos. Group photos and unclear images are not accepted.

### How long does it take to be approved?

Every application is reviewed by the volunteer team, including proof of identity, within 2 working days. Once approved you can browse verified profiles and request introductions from your dashboard.

### What if my application is not approved first time?

If something needs correcting, such as an unclear photo or ID image, you will be told what to fix. You can then correct it and submit your application again for review.

### Can I see profiles before registering?

Yes. You can [browse a preview of profiles](/demo) before you decide to register. The preview shows anonymised cards with no names, photos or contact details. Full profiles, saved lists and contact requests are available to approved members.

## Cost and membership

### How much does membership cost?

Annual membership is £10, payable by card when you register. The register is a not-for-profit service run by the community, for the community. There is no advertising and data is never sold.

### Are there any extra charges or premium upgrades?

No. Every member pays the same £10 a year and has the same features. There are no paid tiers.

### How do I renew?

Your dashboard shows when your membership is due, and you can renew there for £10 by card. If you renew before your membership ends, you get an extra month free.

### Can I get free months by recommending a friend?

Yes. Approved members have a personal code in their dashboard. When a friend you recommend is accepted, you get free months and they get a bonus month too.

### Why is it so cheap?

Because the register is run by volunteers of Vanik Council, a registered charity, to serve the community rather than to make a profit. The fee helps cover the costs of running a secure, verified service.

### Can I pause my membership?

You can pause your profile at any time from your dashboard. Pausing hides you from browse and saved lists, and you can unpause whenever you like. Your membership keeps running while you are paused.

### How do I leave the register?

You can request account deletion from the My profile page in your dashboard. If you have found someone, we would love to hear about it first.

## Safety and privacy

### Is the Vanik Matrimonial Register safe?

Every member is checked by a volunteer, including proof of identity, before their profile appears. Only approved members can browse, and names, photos and contact details are never displayed publicly. Our [safety page](/safety) explains exactly what is shared and when.

### Is my information kept private?

Yes. Contact details are shared only inside your member dashboard when an introduction is made, and nothing that identifies you is ever displayed publicly. Every profile is verified before it appears, and the service does not sell data or show advertising. Read our [privacy policy](/privacy).

### Can other members see my photos and name while browsing?

No. Browsing cards show details such as age, job, where you live, religion, diet and height, but not your name or photos. Your name, photos and full profile are shared only with members you are introduced to.

### Will people know if I look at their profile?

No. Nobody is told that you looked at their profile, and nobody can see who looked at yours.

### Is my home address shared?

No. Your address is used for your application and is not shared with other members. When an introduction is made, members receive each other's name, mobile number and profile, plus a LinkedIn link if one was added.

### Are there fake profiles?

Every application is checked by a volunteer before the profile appears, including proof of identity, so there are no bots or fake accounts.

### What if someone makes me uncomfortable?

Email matrimonial@vanikcouncil.uk and a volunteer will look into it. Members agree to use the register respectfully, and harassment, misuse of contact details or misrepresentation can lead to suspension or removal. You can also pause your profile at any time.

### Is it safe for women?

The register was designed with everyone's safety in mind, and many of its features matter especially to women: every member is ID-checked, there is no public profile, there is no chat inbox, and limits mean nobody can contact large numbers of people. Our [safety page](/safety) and guide to [staying safe](/guides/staying-safe) include practical tips for meeting someone new.

## Profiles and introductions

### How do introductions work?

Once approved, you browse verified profiles and save anyone you would like to know more about. You can then request contact details and view them in your dashboard, up to three at a time, with fair weekly and monthly limits to keep things balanced.

### Does the other person have to accept my request?

No. An introduction is two-way and happens straight away. You both receive each other's name, photos, full profile and mobile number, and they receive an email so they know who to expect to hear from.

### Can I message people through the site?

No. The register does not pass messages on and has no chat inbox. Once introduced, either of you can make the first move by phone or message.

### What are the request limits?

As standard, you can request up to three new people in any rolling 7 days and up to six in a calendar month. You cannot request the same person again within 7 days. These limits keep introductions personal and stop anyone being flooded with requests.

### Why am I asked for feedback after an introduction?

Short feedback within 21 days helps the volunteer team keep the register safe and see what is working. It goes only to the team and is never shown to the other member. If an introduction is more than 21 days old and still has no feedback, new requests pause until you catch up.

### Can I filter profiles?

Yes. You can filter by age, height, diet and religion, and choose whether to show people whose details you already have.

### Can Hindu and Jain members be matched?

Yes. Hindu and Jain members can see each other's profiles, and matches between Gujarati Hindu and Jain families are warmly welcomed. Read our guide to [Hindu and Jain matches](/guides/hindu-jain-matches).

### Can I show that I follow a Jain diet?

Yes. Every profile shows diet: vegetarian, Jain diet, vegan, pescetarian or non-vegetarian. You can also filter by diet. See [Jain matrimonial UK](/jain-matrimonial-uk).

### Can I edit my profile after I join?

Yes. You can update your profile details from your dashboard at any time.

### Any tips for writing a good profile?

Be warm, specific and honest, and choose a clear, recent main photo. Our guide to [writing your profile](/guides/writing-your-profile) has more ideas.

## For parents

### I am a parent. Can I register on behalf of my son or daughter?

We love that parents want to help: many happy matches in our community started exactly that way. The account itself, though, should be created by the person looking to marry. They are the one who will browse profiles, receive introductions and speak with matches, and we verify each member's own identity. The best way to help is to share this site with them and encourage them to register. It only takes a few minutes, and you are very welcome to sit with them while they do.

### How can I help my daughter find a Gujarati husband, or my son find a wife?

Share the register with them, help them write a warm profile and choose good photos, and talk through introductions together while leaving the decision with them. Our page [for parents](/for-parents) and our [parents' guide](/guides/parents-guide) have more ideas.

### How can I reassure my child that it is safe?

Explain that every member is ID-checked by a volunteer, that names, photos and contact details are never shown publicly, and that they choose who to contact. There is no chat inbox and they can pause at any time. Show them the [safety page](/safety).

## Living abroad

### I live outside the UK. Can I join?

Yes. The register is mainly UK-based, and members living abroad are welcome too. You can enter an address outside the UK and a mobile number with your country code when you register.

### Can I say whether I would move to or from the UK?

Yes. Profiles include an optional field for future settlement plans, for example "happy to stay in the UK" or "open to relocating". Other members can see this while browsing.

### Can I pay from abroad?

Membership is paid by card, online, when you register.
