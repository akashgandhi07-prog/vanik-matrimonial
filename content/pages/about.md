The Vanik Matrimonial Register is a not-for-profit matrimonial introduction service for adults from Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, run by volunteers of Vanik Council (UK), a registered charity in England and Wales (number 1137083). It has been running for over 40 years and has led to hundreds of marriages.

## Who runs the register?

The register is run by Vanik Council (UK). The Council was founded in 1978 as The National Council of Vanik Associations (UK) and is a registered charity in England and Wales, charity number 1137083.

Vanik Council is an umbrella organisation. It brings together affiliated associations across the UK, many of which are charities in their own right. Like those associations, the Council is run by volunteers who give their time to serve the wider samaj.

The matrimonial register is one of the ways the Council supports families. Volunteers check every application, look after the register day to day and answer questions from members and parents.

## How long has the register been running?

The Vanik Matrimonial Register has been helping people meet for over 40 years. In that time it has led to hundreds of marriages, and many families in the community can point to a couple who first met this way.

Today the register is a secure online service. The approach is simple: people who are serious about marriage, checked by volunteers, and introduced respectfully.

## Who is it for?

The register is open to adults aged 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, whether or not your family is Vanik. Most members live in the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too.

It is designed for people who are looking for marriage, not casual dating. Singles join themselves, and parents are warmly encouraged to help. You can read more on our page [for parents](/for-parents).

## What does it cost, and why is it not-for-profit?

Membership costs £10 a year, payable by card. There are no paid tiers, no adverts and your data is never sold.

The register is not-for-profit because it exists to serve the community, not to make money from it. The fee helps cover the running costs of a secure, verified service. Because there is no commercial pressure, there is no reason to keep you scrolling, push upgrades or sell your attention to advertisers.

## What makes it different?

- **Every member is checked by a volunteer.** Each application, including proof of identity, is reviewed within 2 working days before the profile appears.
- **Privacy comes first.** Names, photos and contact details are never displayed publicly. Full profiles are only visible to approved members, and contact details only appear inside the member dashboard.
- **Introductions, not endless chatting.** You can have up to three introductions on the go at a time, with fair weekly and monthly limits, so every request means something.
- **Community-specific.** Everyone on the register comes from a Gujarati Hindu or Jain family, so shared background and values are a starting point rather than something you have to filter for.

You can see how this compares with large commercial sites and dating apps on our [comparison page](/compared).

## Does Vanik Council run events too?

Yes. Alongside the online register, Vanik Council runs speed dating events twice a year for people who prefer to meet face to face. See our [events page](/events) for how to hear about the next one.
