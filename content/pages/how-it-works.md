You register and upload proof of identity, a volunteer checks your application within 2 working days, and once approved you can browse verified members and request introductions from your private dashboard. Here is every step in detail, from signing up to renewing.

## Step 1: Create your account and profile

Start at the [registration page](/register). You will create an account with your email address and a password, then confirm your email. The account must be created by the person looking to marry. Parents are very welcome to sit alongside and help (see [for parents](/for-parents)).

The form has three short parts:

- **Identity and address:** gender, date of birth, mobile number, home address and proof of identity. You can also add a LinkedIn profile if you wish.
- **Personal details:** your name, nationality, where you live, your family's town and country of origin, religion, and (optionally) your future settlement plans, for example "happy to stay in the UK" or "open to relocating".
- **Profile and photos:** education, job title, height, diet, hobbies and interests, and up to three photos.

Your main photo should be clear and recent, with your face easy to see. Group photos are not accepted. For help with what to write, read our guide to [writing your profile](/guides/writing-your-profile).

Membership is £10 a year, paid securely by card before you submit. If someone has given you a coupon or referral code, you can enter it on the form.

## Step 2: Verification by a volunteer

Every application is checked by a Vanik Council volunteer, usually within 2 working days. This includes your proof of identity, which can be the photo page of your passport or your driving licence. The ID image is used only to confirm who you are and is deleted after approval.

Volunteers also check that your photos and details are suitable. If something needs correcting, you will be told what to fix and can resubmit. This is why every profile on the register carries a "Verified" mark: you only ever see real people.

## Step 3: Browse profiles

Once approved, you can browse straight away. Profile cards show details such as age, job, where someone lives, religion, diet, height and nationality. You can open a profile to read more, including education, family origin and hobbies.

Browsing is discreet. Cards show no names or photos, and nobody is told that you looked at their profile. You can filter by age, height, diet and religion.

Want to see what it looks like first? [Preview profiles](/demo) before you join.

## Step 4: Save anyone who interests you

Save profiles to a shortlist so you can come back to them later. Saving is private and nothing is sent to the other person.

## Step 5: Request an introduction

When you are ready, add people to your request tray and submit. An introduction on the register is two-way:

- You receive their name and mobile number by email, and their full profile and photos appear under My requests in your dashboard.
- They receive an email telling them you asked, and your full profile, photos and contact details appear in their dashboard.

There is no approval step, so the introduction is made straight away. You can have up to three introductions at a time, with fair weekly and monthly limits. This keeps things personal and stops anyone sending requests to everyone.

## Step 6: Get in touch and meet

The register does not pass messages on. Either of you can make the first move, by phone or message, and take it from there: a call, a coffee, a proper conversation. Our guides on [the first meeting](/guides/first-meeting) and [questions to ask before marriage](/guides/questions-to-ask-before-marriage) may help.

## Step 7: Tell us how it went

After each introduction you will be asked for brief feedback within 21 days. It goes only to the volunteer team and is never shown to the other member. It helps keep the register safe and tells us what is working.

## Pausing your profile

Busy, travelling, or getting to know someone? You can pause your profile at any time from your dashboard. Pausing hides you from browse and saved lists, and your membership keeps running. People who already have your details can still see you in their requests. You can unpause whenever you like.

If you find someone, we would love to hear about it. When you pause, you can tell us whether you met through the register.

## Renewing your membership

Membership lasts a year. Your dashboard shows when it is due and you can renew there for £10. If you renew before your membership ends, you get an extra month free.

You can also recommend a friend with your personal code: you get free months for every friend who is accepted, and they get a bonus month too.
