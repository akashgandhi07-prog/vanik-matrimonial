Vanik Council runs speed dating events twice a year for singles from Gujarati Hindu and Jain families who would like to meet people face to face. To hear about the next event, email matrimonial@vanikcouncil.uk.

## What is speed dating?

Speed dating is a simple way to meet several people in one session. Instead of one long first date, you have a series of short conversations, each lasting a few minutes. When time is up, everyone moves on to the next conversation.

Usually, you then note who you would like to see again. The idea is that a few minutes in person tells you more than days of messages, and there is no awkwardness about saying no.

Each event's details, including date, venue and price, are shared when it is announced. If you would like a sense of what an event can feel like, read our guide to [a speed dating night](/guides/speed-dating-night).

## Who are the events for?

The events are for single adults from Gujarati Hindu and Jain families who are thinking about marriage. They suit people who:

- prefer meeting in person to browsing profiles online
- find it easier to judge chemistry face to face
- would like to meet several people without arranging lots of separate meetings
- are new to the community scene, or have moved to a new area
- want a relaxed, social way to start

Anyone is welcome to email and ask about an event, and the events and the online register work well together.

## Why meet in person?

Profiles are useful, but they cannot show how someone laughs, listens or makes you feel at ease. Meeting face to face lets you get a feel for that quickly.

Speed dating also takes away much of the pressure. Everyone is there for the same reason, the conversations are short, and you are meeting in a shared, social setting. For people who feel shy about approaching someone, or who find online browsing tiring, it can be a more natural way to begin.

## Is it suitable if I am nervous or new to this?

Yes. Many people arrive feeling a little nervous, and that is completely normal. A few tips:

- Have two or three easy questions ready, such as what someone enjoys doing at weekends.
- Listen as much as you talk.
- Be yourself rather than trying to impress.
- Remember that each conversation is only a few minutes long.

If you are in your 20s and starting to think about marriage, our guide on [finding a partner in your 20s](/guides/finding-a-partner-in-your-20s) has more ideas.
