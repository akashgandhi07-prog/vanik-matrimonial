The Vanik Matrimonial Register is a not-for-profit introduction service for Jain singles and families in the UK, run by volunteers of Vanik Council (registered charity 1137083). Every member is checked by a volunteer, including proof of identity, and membership costs £10 a year.

## Is this a Jain matrimonial service?

Yes. The register is open to adults aged 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, whether or not your family is Vanik. The register has been running for over 40 years and has led to hundreds of marriages.

Because everyone on the register comes from a Gujarati Hindu or Jain background, shared culture and values are a starting point. You are not searching through thousands of unrelated profiles to find people who understand your way of life.

## Can I find someone who follows a Jain diet?

Yes. Diet is part of every profile, and members choose from vegetarian, Jain diet, vegan, pescetarian or non-vegetarian. You can see each person's diet on their profile card while browsing, and you can filter the register by diet.

For many Jain families, food is about much more than preference. It reflects ahimsa and daily practice, and it shapes family life, festivals and the kitchen at home. Seeing diet up front means you can focus on the people whose everyday life is likely to fit with yours, and avoid awkward conversations later.

## Can I search for Jain members only?

Every profile shows religion, and members choose Jain, Hindu or other. You can filter by religion as well as by diet, age and height. Many Jain members prefer to keep their search open to Gujarati Hindu members too, and the register makes it easy to do either.

## Are Hindu and Jain matches welcome?

Yes. Gujarati Hindu and Jain families have long shared language, food, festivals and community life, and many happy marriages bring the two together. Hindu and Jain members can see each other's profiles, and introductions between them are warmly welcomed.

If you or your family are weighing up a Hindu and Jain match, our guide to [Hindu and Jain matches](/guides/hindu-jain-matches) covers the conversations worth having early, from diet to festivals.

## How is this different from a large matrimonial website?

- **Not-for-profit.** It is run by community volunteers, not a company. There are no paid tiers and no adverts, and your data is never sold.
- **Verified by real people.** A volunteer checks every application, including passport or driving licence, within 2 working days.
- **Private.** Names, photos and contact details are never displayed publicly. While browsing, members see details such as age, job, diet and where someone lives, but not names or photos.
- **Introductions, not endless chatting.** You can have up to three introductions at a time, with fair weekly and monthly limits.

Our [comparison page](/compared) sets out the differences in more detail.

## Do I need to live in London?

No. The register is for Jain families wherever they live in the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too. Every profile shows where someone lives, and members can add their future settlement plans, for example whether they would be happy to relocate. See our guide to [matches beyond London](/guides/matches-beyond-london).

## What will I be asked when I register?

Alongside the usual details, the form asks for your religion, diet, family's town and country of origin, education, job, height, hobbies and up to three photos. You also upload proof of identity, which is used only for the volunteer check and deleted after approval. The [how it works](/how-it-works) page walks through each step.

## Can my parents help?

Yes, and we encourage it. The account must be created by the person looking to marry, but parents are very welcome to share the link and sit with them while they register. Our page [for parents](/for-parents) explains how families can help.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is there a Jain matrimonial service in London or Leicester?

Yes. The Vanik Matrimonial Register is a UK-wide register for Jain and Gujarati Hindu families, run by Vanik Council volunteers. It is not tied to one city, so you can join whether you live in London, Leicester or anywhere else in the UK, and every profile shows where someone lives.

### How much does a Jain matrimonial service cost?

The Vanik Matrimonial Register costs £10 a year. It is not-for-profit, with no paid tiers and no adverts, and your data is never sold.

### Is the register only for Vanik Jain families?

No. It is run by Vanik Council, but it is open to adults 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, whether or not your family is Vanik.

### Can I find a Jain partner who lives abroad?

The register is mainly for families in the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too. Members can add their future settlement plans, so it is easy to see who is open to moving.

### Are there Jain singles events in the UK?

Vanik Council runs speed dating events twice a year for Gujarati Hindu and Jain singles. Details are announced by email; see [events](/events) or email matrimonial@vanikcouncil.uk to hear about the next one.
