The Vanik Matrimonial Register is a not-for-profit introduction service for Gujarati Hindu singles and families in the UK, run by volunteers of Vanik Council (registered charity 1137083). Every member is checked by a volunteer, including proof of identity, and membership costs £10 a year with no adverts.

## Who can join?

The register is open to adults aged 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families. You do not need to be from a Vanik family to join. Most members live in the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too.

The register has been running for over 40 years and has led to hundreds of marriages. It is designed for people who are looking for marriage, not casual dating.

## Why choose a community register?

Large matrimonial websites cover every community and every country. That brings huge numbers, but it also means a lot of searching to find people who share your background. On this register, everyone comes from a Gujarati Hindu or Jain family, so a shared language, food, festivals and family values are already common ground.

Many families also value the feeling that comes with a register run by the samaj itself: volunteers who understand the community, a modest annual fee, and no commercial reason to keep anyone scrolling.

## What will I see on a profile?

Profiles are designed to show what matters for marriage. While browsing, cards show age, job, where someone lives, religion, diet, height and nationality, plus any future settlement plans. Opening a profile shows education, family's town and country of origin, and hobbies and interests.

Family origin is often a helpful starting point for conversation, whether a family's roots are in Gujarat, East Africa or elsewhere. Settlement plans help you see early on whether someone is happy to stay in their part of the UK or open to relocating.

You can filter by age, height, diet and religion. Names and photos are not shown while browsing, and photos appear once an introduction is made.

## Can a Gujarati Hindu marry someone from a Jain family through the register?

Yes. Hindu and Jain members can see each other's profiles, and matches between Gujarati Hindu and Jain families are warmly welcomed. Many families have links across both, and the register reflects that. If diet matters to you, each profile shows whether someone is vegetarian, follows a Jain diet, is vegan, pescetarian or non-vegetarian. Our guide to [Hindu and Jain matches](/guides/hindu-jain-matches) has more.

## How are members verified?

A Vanik Council volunteer checks every application within 2 working days, including the photo page of a passport or a driving licence. The ID image is used only for this check and deleted after approval. Photos and profile details are checked too. There are no bots and no fake accounts. Read more on our [safety page](/safety).

## How do introductions work?

Once approved, you browse and save anyone who interests you. When you are ready, you request an introduction. Both of you then receive each other's name, photos, full profile and mobile number, and you take it from there with a call or a coffee.

You can have up to three introductions at a time, with fair weekly and monthly limits. There is no chat inbox, so the focus stays on real conversations. The full walkthrough is on [how it works](/how-it-works).

## I do not live in North-West London. Is it still for me?

Yes. The register is for Gujarati Hindu families wherever they live in the UK, and members abroad are welcome too. Every profile shows where someone lives, so you can see who is nearby or who is open to moving. Our guide to [matches beyond London](/guides/matches-beyond-london) has ideas for making distance work.

## I am in my 20s. Is it too early?

Not at all. Many people start thinking seriously about marriage in their 20s, and a verified, low-pressure register is a calm way to begin. There is no pressure to request anyone until you are ready. See our guide on [finding a partner in your 20s](/guides/finding-a-partner-in-your-20s).

## Can my parents register for me?

The account must be created by the person looking to marry, because they will browse, receive introductions and speak to matches, and we verify their own identity. Parents are encouraged to share the link and sit with them while they register. Our page [for parents](/for-parents) explains how families can help.
