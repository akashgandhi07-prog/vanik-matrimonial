Parents cannot register on behalf of a son or daughter, but you can help in many ways: share the link, sit with them while they sign up, and talk through introductions together. The account itself must be created by the person looking to marry, because they browse, receive introductions and speak to matches, and we verify their own identity.

## Why does my son or daughter need to register themselves?

Many happy matches in our community started with a parent's encouragement, and we love that families want to help. The account, though, belongs to the person who is looking to marry, for three simple reasons:

- **Identity checks.** A volunteer verifies each member's own proof of identity. The person on the profile must be the person who registered.
- **Introductions are direct.** When an introduction is made, contact details go straight to the two members, who then speak to each other.
- **It works better.** People respond more warmly to a profile written in someone's own words, by someone who is ready to take part.

The best thing you can do is share the link and encourage them. Registering only takes a few minutes, and you are very welcome to sit with them while they do.

## How can I share the register with my child?

Send them the link to [the registration page](/register), or start with the [free profile preview](/demo) so they can see the kind of people on the register before deciding. If they have questions, this site's [how it works](/how-it-works) and [FAQ](/faq) pages explain everything in plain English.

A gentle conversation often helps more than a long one. Some ideas:

- Mention that it is run by Vanik Council volunteers, not a company.
- Explain that it costs £10 a year with no upgrades or adverts.
- Reassure them that they decide who to contact and when.
- Offer to help with the profile or photos, then step back.

## What can I reassure my son or daughter about?

Young people are often wary of matrimonial sites. These are the points that tend to matter most.

**It is private.** Names, photos and contact details are never displayed publicly. While browsing, members see details such as age, job and where someone lives, but no names or photos. Nobody is told who looked at their profile.

**Everyone is verified.** Every application, including proof of identity, is checked by a volunteer within 2 working days. There are no fake profiles or bots. Our [safety page](/safety) explains what members can and cannot see.

**There is no pressure.** Members choose who to request, and limits mean nobody is flooded with contact. They can pause their profile at any time if they need a break.

**It is for serious people.** Everyone on the register is looking for marriage, not casual dating. There is no endless chatting.

**It is not only for one area.** The register is open to Gujarati Hindu and Jain families across the UK, and members living abroad are welcome too. Profiles show where someone lives and whether they are open to relocating. See our guide to [matches beyond London](/guides/matches-beyond-london).

## How can I help once they have joined?

- **Help with the profile.** A warm, honest description and a clear, recent photo make a real difference. Our guide to [writing your profile](/guides/writing-your-profile) has tips.
- **Talk through introductions.** Your experience is valuable. Many families like to talk over a new introduction together, while leaving the decision with their child.
- **Encourage a relaxed first meeting.** A coffee or a walk is less pressure than a formal family meeting. See [the first meeting](/guides/first-meeting).
- **Suggest the events.** Vanik Council runs speed dating twice a year, which suits people who prefer meeting face to face. See [events](/events).

Our longer [parents' guide](/guides/parents-guide) covers these ideas in more depth, including how to raise the subject.

## Does my son or daughter have to be from a Vanik family?

No. The register is open to adults aged 18 and over from all Gujarati Hindu and Jain families, Vanik or not. Hindu and Jain members can see each other's profiles and matches between the two are welcome. Read more for [Jain families](/jain-matrimonial-uk) and [Gujarati Hindu families](/gujarati-hindu-matrimonial-uk).

## Can I speak to someone first?

Of course. Email the volunteer team at matrimonial@vanikcouncil.uk with any questions. We are always happy to hear from parents and the wider parivar who want to understand the register before suggesting it.
